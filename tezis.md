## 1. The big idea

**The first PyCon in the mountains of Tajikistan.**
Tajikistan is joining the global Python family. We are building a home for everyone who writes, learns or teaches Python here, and opening a door between Dushanbe and the world.

## 3. Six core messages (tezis)

1. **It's the first.** PyCon Tajikistan 2026 is the first PyCon in the country. Being there means being part of the story from the start.
2. **It's for everyone.** Students, beginners, teachers, professionals. No one is "too new" to come.
3. **It's practical.** Real talks, hands-on workshops, real projects by local students. You leave knowing something new.
4. **It's ours, and global.** Talks in Tajik, Russian and English. Local speakers next to guests from abroad.
5. **It's community-run and not for profit.** Volunteers organise it, sponsors keep tickets cheap, and student tickets are subsidised.
6. **It's safe and welcoming.** A clear code of conduct, a team to help, a prayer room, a quiet room. Tajik hospitality, Python style.

## 4. Voice and tone

- **Warm, like a host.** "Come in, have some tea, let's talk Python."
- **Simple.** Short sentences. Explain jargon. Many readers are beginners or reading in their second language.
- **Confident, not boastful.** We are new, and we say so with pride.
- **A little playful.** Mountain and tea jokes are fine. Never at anyone's expense.
- **Never:** corporate buzzwords, jokes about other programming languages, stereotypes.

- Text: PyCon is a worldwide family of community conferences about the Python language. Now it has a home in Tajikistan. Come to learn, share what you built, and meet the people who will build Tajikistan's tech future with you.

- **Title:** You made it to the summit.
- **Text:** One ticket, one day, a hundred new friends. Student tickets are subsidised by our sponsors.
- **Pay it forward:** "Add a donation to give a free ticket to a student who can't afford one."

### 6.8 Speak (call for proposals)
- **Title:** Share what you know
- **Text:** You don't need to be an expert. If you learned something with Python, someone in the hall wants to hear it. Talks can be in Tajik, Russian or English.
- **Formats:** Talk (25 min + 5 min questions) · Workshop (90 min, hands-on) · Lightning talk (5 min, sign up on the day)
- **Topics:** web, data and AI, education, robotics and hardware, open source, Python in business and government, your first project, anything else.
- **Support:** "First time speaking? We'll pair you with a mentor."
- **Rules:** Write your proposal yourself. Proposals written mostly by AI will not be accepted.

### 6.9 Tickets
- Student: For school pupils and students, with ID.
- Personal: For people paying for themselves.
- Business / Supporter: When your employer pays. You help pay for student tickets.
- Pay it forward: A donation that becomes someone else's ticket.
- **Included:** all talks and workshops, lunch, tea and coffee all day, a sticker pack and your personal suzani badge.

### 6.10 Code of conduct (summary)
- **Title:** Everyone is welcome here
- **Text:** PyCon Tajikistan is for everyone, whatever your gender, age, religion, language, background or level of experience. Harassment of any kind is not allowed. If something happens, talk to any organiser in a yellow T-shirt, or write to {coc email}. We will listen and act.

### 6.11 Venue

- Short practical blocks: address, map, public transport and taxi, prayer room, quiet room, accessibility, food (all halal).
