{% translate "Dushanbe" %}

{% translate "Dushanbe is a green, walkable capital with wide avenues, plane trees and mountains on the horizon. Most places worth seeing are on or near Rudaki Avenue." %}

{% translate "Rudaki Avenue and Rudaki Park" %}
{% translate "The main street. Walk it in the evening, when the fountains are on and families are out." %}
{% translate "National Museum of Tajikistan" %}
{% translate "History and art from ancient Sogdiana to today, in one large modern building." %}
{% translate "Navruz Palace" %}
{% translate "Halls of carved wood, painted ceilings and stone, made by craftsmen from across the country." %}
{% translate "Mehrgon market" %}
{% translate "Dried apricots, nuts, spices, fresh bread and fruit. The best place to buy gifts to take home." %}

{% translate "Food" %}

{% translate "Tajik food is made for sharing. Meals start with bread and tea and can go on for hours." %}

{% translate "Qurutob" %}
{% translate "The national dish: flatbread soaked in qurut, a salty sauce of dried yogurt, topped with onions and herbs. Eaten by hand from a shared wooden bowl." %}
{% translate "Oshi palav" %}
{% translate "Plov: rice, carrots and meat cooked slowly in a big cauldron. The dish for guests and celebrations." %}
{% translate "Sambusa" %}
{% translate "Pastries filled with meat and onion, baked on the wall of a clay oven." %}
{% translate "Non" %}
{% translate "Round bread with a stamped pattern. It is treated with respect: never put it upside down or throw it away." %}
{% translate "Green tea" %}
{% translate "Served all day in small bowls called piyola. The host keeps refilling, so a small portion is a good sign." %}

{% translate "Culture" %}

{% translate "Tajiks are famous for hospitality. If you are invited for tea, say yes." %}

  • {% translate "People greet with a hand on the heart. Салом (salom) means hello, and раҳмат (rahmat) means thank you." %}
  • {% translate "Take your shoes off before you sit on a tapchan, the raised wooden platform in teahouses." %}
  • {% translate "Ask before you photograph people. Most will say yes." %}
  • {% translate "Dress modestly when you visit mosques and shrines." %}
  • {% translate "Tajik is a Persian language, written in Cyrillic. Many people also speak Russian, and younger people often speak English." %}

{% translate "Day trips" %}

{% translate "Varzob gorge" %}
{% translate "30 to 60 minutes north by car. A cold mountain river, fresh air and teahouses where you eat on platforms over the water. Half a day is enough." %}
{% translate "Hisor fortress" %}
{% translate "About 30 km west of Dushanbe. A restored fortress gate, an old madrasa and a small museum. Half a day." %}
{% translate "Iskanderkul" %}
{% translate "A turquoise lake at about 2,200 m in the Fann Mountains, around three to four hours by car. Plan a long day, or stay a night." %}

{% translate "A hike: Gusgarf waterfall" %}

{% translate "In the Varzob valley, a path climbs a side gorge to the Gusgarf waterfall. It is an easy half-day walk on a rocky trail. Take water, sun protection and good shoes, and go with a local guide or friends who know the way. Ask in our community chat and someone will likely join you." %}
